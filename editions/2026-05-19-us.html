PSL Demo Day: PolicyEngine’s TAXSIM Emulator

Since 1977, the National Bureau of Economic Research has supported government agencies and over 1,000 academic papers through TAXSIM, its free state-and-federal individual income tax calculator. PolicyEngine is now stewarding TAXSIM’s future by building an open-source emulator powered by our Python tax-benefit microsimulation model. Hear from TAXSIM creator Dan Feenberg and PolicyEngine chief of staff Pavel Makarchuk on what we’ve built, where it’s headed, and what this means for public finance research.

New Dashboard

South Carolina 2026 Tax Changes Calculator

South Carolina’s H.4216 (Act 110), signed March 30, 2026, restructures the state income tax: it consolidates the three-bracket schedule into two brackets, replaces federal deductions with a state-specific SCIAD deduction, and caps the nonrefundable state EITC at $200. The dashboard lets you explore the household, distributional, and statewide impacts.
