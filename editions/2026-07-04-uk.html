New Analysis · Progress and Poverty

How replacing council tax with a flat land value tax would affect households in the UK

A guest post for Progress and Poverty by Vahid Ahmadi and Max Ghenis: what if Britain scrapped council tax and taxed land instead, at the same revenue?

What we find

• A budget-neutral 0.77% LVT on the UK’s £7.5 trillion of land value replaces council tax.
• 68% of households gain: the bottom income decile by £481/yr, while the top two deciles pay up to £991 more.
• Absolute poverty (before housing costs) falls 0.65pp.
