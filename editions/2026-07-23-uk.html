Bus fares · Electricity VAT · Employer NICs

Four new UK analysis dashboards

This edition: interactive analyses of the £2 bus fare cap, the temporary VAT cut on domestic electricity, and two employer NICs exemptions — for recently-inactive workers and for young workers.

New Dashboard · Transport

£2 bus fare cap analysis

The government announced that the single bus fare cap falls from £3 to £2 for calendar 2027, on participating services in England outside London.

What we find

• Against current-law funding expiry the cost is £409m, close to the government’s £400m of England funding.
• 14.3m people live in the policy geography; average household saving is £18 a year.

New Dashboard · Energy

Temporary VAT cut on domestic electricity

The government announced a cut to VAT on domestic electricity from 5% to 0% for six months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.

What we find

• The six-month cost to households is £669m, below the government’s £850m claim — which also covers small businesses, charities and care homes on the domestic rate.
• The mean gain is £43 a year on a full-year basis — close to the government’s £45 price-cap figure.
• As a share of household income, the gain is 3.8× larger for the bottom quintile than the top.

New Dashboard · Labour market

NICs exemption for recently-inactive employees

Exempting employers from NICs on workers who recently moved from economic inactivity into work.

What we find

• Static cost of £4.8bn a year on 1.8m recently-active employees.
• That is £2,709 of employer NICs per exempt hire.

New Dashboard · Labour market

Employer NICs exemption for young workers

Extending the employer NICs zero rate — already covering under-21s and young apprentices — to young workers, either all 18 to 24-year-olds or only recent NEETs.

What we find

• Targeting recent NEETs and excluding public-sector employers, the static cost in the relieved band is £0.71bn in 2027-28.
• 219k recent-NEET employed 21 to 24-year-olds are newly exempt.
• Average employer NICs saving per newly exempt employee is £3,257.
