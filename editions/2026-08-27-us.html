The Child Poverty Impact Dashboard: modeling child poverty reforms in every state

David Trimmer and Max Ghenis walk through PolicyEngine’s new interactive tool for scoring child poverty reforms across all 50 states and DC, with guest speakers Cynthia Osborne (Prenatal-to-3 Policy Impact Center) and Joshua McCabe (Niskanen Center).

Why join

What would a state Child Tax Credit, an expanded EITC, or a child allowance do to child poverty in your state? The Child Poverty Impact Dashboard is a new interactive tool from PolicyEngine that answers that question in minutes: configure a reform — state CTCs and EITCs, dependent exemptions, child allowances, SNAP expansions, or federal proposals like the American Family Act — and see its impact on child poverty, state and federal budgets, income distribution, congressional districts, and individual households.

In this session, we’ll demo the dashboard live — building reforms, tracing one family’s taxes and benefits across the earnings range, and comparing statewide and district-level impacts. We’ll cover what’s modeled, how the estimates are produced by PolicyEngine’s open-source microsimulation model over newly calibrated state and local-area microdata, and how researchers, advocates, and legislative staff can use it.
