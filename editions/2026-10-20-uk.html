New Dashboard · Childcare

Scrapping the childcare cliff edge

Working parents in England lose the 30 funded hours, and parents across the UK lose Tax-Free Childcare, once either parent’s adjusted net income goes over £100,000. The Conservatives have pledged to remove that limit; our new dashboard costs the change.

What we find

•Before any change in how much parents work, removing the limit would cost £0.59bn in 2027-28, rising to £0.68bn by 2029-30.
•In 2027-28, 278,000 families would gain £2,132 a year on average.

New Dashboard · Cost of living

The Middle East war and UK living standards

Our dashboard stress-tests how higher energy, fuel and food prices linked to the Middle East war would affect UK households in 2027-28 under three price scenarios, and compares nine policy responses and a combined package.

What we find

•If prices stayed at summer 2026 levels through 2027-28, households would pay £538 a year more on average than at pre-conflict prices.
•For the bottom income quintile, the extra cost equals 1.65% of average household net income before housing costs, against 0.60% for the top quintile.
