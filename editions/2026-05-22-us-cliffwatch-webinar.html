A live demo of CliffWatch: where, when, and how benefit cliffs hit US households

Daphne Hansell and Max Ghenis walk through PolicyEngine’s new interactive tool for mapping benefit cliffs across all 50 states and DC.

Why join

Benefit cliffs—where a small earnings increase reduces a household’s benefits by more than the earnings gain—are difficult to anticipate from program rules alone. CliffWatch is a new interactive tool from PolicyEngine that lets you see where, when, and how cliffs occur for any US household, across all 50 states and DC.

In this session, we’ll demo CliffWatch live—building a household, walking across earnings levels, and comparing benefit-cliff exposure between states. We’ll cover what’s modeled, how the underlying calculations work, and how researchers, advocates, and caseworkers can use it.

What CliffWatch models

SNAP, TANF, WIC, free school meals, Head Start, Early Head Start, child care subsidies, housing assistance, SSI, Medicaid, CHIP, ACA premium tax credits, and federal/state/local refundable tax credits—everything in the PolicyEngine US model.
