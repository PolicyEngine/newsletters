Computable law for all.

Axiom Foundation Launch

Tuesday, July 28, 2026 · 1:00 PM ET

Online · ~30 minutes + live Q&A

The rules that decide who gets food assistance, health coverage, and tax credits live in closed code that no one can check — re-implemented privately inside every system that needs them.

On Tuesday, July 28, the Axiom Foundation goes public to change that. Axiom publishes the open, verifiable layer for computable law: statutes, regulations, and policy rules turned into cited, time-aware, executable code anyone can run, audit, or reform.

At the launch briefing we’ll walk through the platform, show how every encoded rule traces back to statute, explore the application layers that can be built on top, and take your questions.
