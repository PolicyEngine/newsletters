ACA benchmark premiums rise again in 2027

Our updated ACA calculator shows what your household would pay for the benchmark plan in 2026 and 2027 under current law, and under H.R. 1834, which would revive the enhanced premium tax credits.

The calculator for a 40-year-old in Harris County, Texas: what they would pay for the benchmark plan at each level of earnings.

The benchmark plan is the second-lowest-cost silver plan in the area where you live. The premium tax credit covers the gap between its premium and the amount the law expects your household to pay, which depends on your income.

For example, a 40-year-old in Harris County, Texas, earning $40,000 sees the benchmark premium rise $88 to $770 a month in 2027. The credit grows with it, so they pay $289 a month, up $3 from 2026. Under H.R. 1834 they would pay $133.

A 45-year-old in Lebanon County, Pennsylvania, earning $104,000 has income above 400% of the poverty line and receives no credit. They pay the full $1,236 a month in 2027, up $109 from 2026. Under H.R. 1834 they would get a $499 credit and pay $737.
